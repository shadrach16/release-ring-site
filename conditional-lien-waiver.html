Conditional lien waiver

Where it sits in Arizona A.R.S. 32-1183(B)

A conditional lien waiver is a signed form that says a lien right is given up once a payment arrives. It is one paper in the closeout package.

Arizona A.R.S. 32-1183(B)

The public text sets a 7-day period after the contractor receives a retention release or final payment. It applies only when the subcontractor gave a billing or invoice and a conditional lien waiver. Without that waiver on file, the clock in Release Ring shows "Waiting for your conditional waiver" instead of a date.

Source: https://www.azleg.gov/ars/32/01183.htm, read 2026-11-19.

What to keep with it

  • The conditional waiver you gave, with its date
  • The billing it came with
  • The final unconditional waiver, when payment arrives

Release Ring files each paper by job and date, so the waiver sits next to the rest of the package.
