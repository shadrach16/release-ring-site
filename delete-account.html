Delete your data

Effective: 2026-11-21

Release Ring has no account, so there is no account to delete. Everything the app keeps is on your phone, and you can remove it yourself. The app is made by Wao Apps. Contact: support.retinue@gmail.com.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Release Ring.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data, then Delete everything.

This removes your jobs, papers and their photos, packets, reminders, settings and any creator code from the phone. It cannot be undone. Export your papers first if you want a copy.

Without the app

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Release Ring → Storage → Clear data.

By email

Email support.retinue@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and we will tell you what, if anything, we hold. We hold no papers or job data: they never leave your phone.

Purchases

Deleting data does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Play Store: Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google Play and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies.
