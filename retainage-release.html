Retainage release

What the closeout package holds, and where the clocks come from

Retainage is the part of each payment the general contractor holds back until the work is done. On many jobs the final part comes back only when the subcontractor's closeout package is complete.

What a closeout package usually holds

  • Punch list sign-off
  • As-builts
  • Warranty
  • O&M manual
  • Test report
  • Final inspection
  • Final unconditional waiver
  • Conditional waiver, final
  • Sub-tier waivers
  • W-9
  • Certificate of insurance
  • Attic stock

Your contract's closeout section lists the papers for your job. Release Ring turns that list into a checklist, shows what is still missing, and builds one PDF with every paper and its date.

Where the release clocks come from

  • California Civil Code 8814(a): 10 days after the direct contractor receives retention. Public text, read 2026-11-19.
  • Arizona A.R.S. 32-1182(H) and (I): 14 days to deemed approval, then 7 days. Public text, read 2026-11-19.
  • Arizona A.R.S. 32-1183(B): 7 days, when the billing and a conditional lien waiver were given. Public text, read 2026-11-19.

Rules change and have conditions. Read the text and your contract before you count on a date.
