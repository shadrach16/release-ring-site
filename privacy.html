Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-21

This policy explains what Release Ring (the "app", Android package com.retinue.releasering) does with your information. The app is made by Wao Apps ("we", "us"). Contact: support.retinue@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • Your jobs, papers, photos and dollar figures stay on your phone. There is no account and no server that holds them.
  • The app has no analytics SDK and no ads, and does not use your advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • The only data that leaves the phone is what the subscription needs, listed in section 3.

2. Stored on your phone

Jobs, the papers you file (photos and PDFs), packets, clock dates, reminders, settings and a few simple counters (for example the date you first opened the app) live in the app's private storage. The counters never leave the phone and are not shared with us or anyone else. Reading a paper (suggesting its slot) happens on the phone. Uninstalling the app, or "Delete my data" in Settings, removes all of it.

3. What leaves the phone

  • Purchases. Payment is handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm a purchase and unlock Pro. RevenueCat receives an app user id it creates (not your name or email), your purchase history for the app and the purchase status. We never see your card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.
  • A creator code, when you have one. If you came from a creator's link, or typed a creator's code, the app keeps the code on your phone and tells our creator program service that the code was used (the code, how it arrived, the app's name and a random install number made on your phone). The code is also stored with RevenueCat so the creator can be paid. A code never unlocks Pro by itself: Pro comes only from a Google Play purchase. Without a code, none of this happens.
  • The packet, only when you share it. When you tap Email packet or Share, the PDF goes to the app you pick (your mail app, for example) and from there to the address you choose. We never receive it.

Like any web request, these reach the services with your network address. Data is sent over HTTPS.

4. Camera, photos and notifications

The app uses the camera or your files only when you tap Take photo or Import PDF. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; if you allow notifications, they come from the phone, not from our server.

5. What we do not do

No analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, no advertising ID, no precise location, no access to your contacts, no selling of personal data.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. There is no account. Use Settings → Delete my data, or see Delete your data. Google Play and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

7. Security

Information sent between the app and its services uses HTTPS. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

8. Children

Release Ring is for working contractors and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's information.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the NDPA in Nigeria, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information. Email support.retinue@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
