Closeout documents

A checklist in plain text

Most general contractors ask for the same papers at the end of a subcontract. Your contract lists the exact set.

Commercial closeout checklist

  1. Punch list sign-off
  2. As-builts
  3. Warranty
  4. O&M manual
  5. Test report
  6. Final inspection
  7. Final unconditional waiver
  8. Conditional waiver, final
  9. Sub-tier waivers
  10. W-9
  11. Certificate of insurance
  12. Attic stock

By trade

  • Painting: paint schedule, product data sheets
  • Electrical: panel schedule, final electrical inspection
  • Mechanical and HVAC: test and balance report, start-up sheets
  • Plumbing: pressure test, backflow test

Release Ring has these lists built in, or reads the closeout page of your spec and adds the items it names.
